# Phase 1 — Business Understanding

**Question answered:** *What problem does PULSE solve, for whom, and how does
the marketplace work?*

The business problem, the operating model and the vocabulary of the project,
plus the two reference files every later phase relies on. The narrative
documents were written last, so they can point to the answers found in
Phases 2–12.

## In one sentence

PULSE has enough partners overall but not in the right place at the right
time: shortages can be forecast a week ahead, repositioning recovers 13% of
lost jobs at no net cost, and bonuses pay only as a surge tool on rain and
festival days.
